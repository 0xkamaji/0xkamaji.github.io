This is the first post on the site, and it is mainly here to prove the publishing flow.

For future writeups, a reliable structure is:

## Suggested template

1. What the sample or challenge is
2. What tools you used
3. What the execution flow looks like
4. Key reversing notes
5. IOCs, ATT&CK mapping, and defender takeaways

## Sample snippet

```text
SHA256: <hash here>
Type: DLL / EXE / document / script
Goal: Initial triage and behavior summary
```

Keep live malware, secrets, and anything unsafe to redistribute out of the repository. Screenshots, hashes,
sanitized excerpts, and your analysis notes are usually the right public artifacts.
